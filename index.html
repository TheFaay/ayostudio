Answers & etiquette

Good to know.

A few details to make planning your visit easier.

Is my appointment confirmed right away?

No. This form sends a booking request. Your time isn’t held until Ayo follows up and confirms availability.

How do I change or cancel?

Please contact the studio using the booking confirmation details once your appointment is confirmed. The studio’s cancellation policy will be shared directly.

How long will my appointment take?

Estimated durations are listed beside each service. Your stylist will discuss the plan with you before getting started.

Do I need a consultation first?

A consultation is available for 30 minutes at no charge. It can be a useful first step for a new look or a more involved service.

What if I’m running late?

Please contact the studio as soon as you can. Contact details will be provided with your appointment confirmation.
