Answers & Etiquette

Good to know.

Common questions about private appointments, studio etiquette, and our personalized hair care process.

mark_email_unread
Need a custom request? Text Ayo at (718) 555-0192
Is my appointment confirmed right away? expand_more

Not immediately. Because Ayo operates as a dedicated one-chair private studio, every request is reviewed manually to ensure sufficient time for your hair's density and length. You will receive an official confirmation and calendar invite within 4 hours.

How do I change or cancel? expand_more

You can reschedule or cancel up to 48 hours prior to your scheduled time directly from your confirmation email link without any fee. Late cancellations within 48 hours forfeit the reservation deposit.

How long will my appointment take? expand_more

Our service times are generous and unhurried. Standard haircuts take 75 minutes, blowouts 45 minutes, while custom color and intricate protective braids range from 2.5 to 4 hours. We never rush to seat the next client.

Do I need a consultation first? expand_more

For standard cuts and blowouts, a quick 10-minute consultation is included in your appointment window. For extensive color corrections or virgin protective styles, we recommend booking our complimentary 30-minute consultation first.

What if I'm running late? expand_more

Please send a text to our direct studio line. We provide a 15-minute grace period. Because we do not double-book or overlap clients, late arrivals beyond 15 minutes may require adjusting the service scope to preserve the next guest's schedule.
