## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-fashion editorial craftsmanship, tactile luxury, and modern architectural warmth. Designed for a bespoke salon and hair wellness studio, it rejects generic clinical minimalism in favor of rich, organic sensory depth. The tone bridges haute couture publishing with grounded artisanal warmth.

### Visual Pillars
- **Organic Gravitas:** Heavy, grounding espresso bases paired with velvety terracotta accents create an intimate, sanctuary-like digital salon atmosphere.
- **Editorial Contrast:** Razor-sharp, avant-garde geometric headlines stand against warm tactile paper textures, champagne translucency, and quiet negative space.
- **Liquid Tactility:** Subtly warmed glass surfaces, luminous amber glows, and micro-refractions that mimic luxury perfume flacons and salon oils.

## Colors

The palette draws entirely from natural hair undertones, terracotta ceramic basins, parchment consultation cards, and roasted espresso tones.

- **Primary (`#9B422B` - Cinnamon Terracotta):** Used for focal calls to action, active booking indicators, artisanal badges, and bespoke selection outlines.
- **Secondary (`#E9DFCE` - Warm Champagne Linen):** Serves as an elevated container tint, subtle hover fill, and softly lustrous pill highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#1C1917` - Deep Espresso Truffle):** Anchor color for high-fashion editorial hero sections, dark mode cards, footers, and primary typography.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Alabaster Silk):** The master surface foundation, yielding an editorial print-magazine tactile feel.
- **Muted Accent (`#786F66` - Warm Taupe Silt):** Strict utility color for metadata, category labels, timestamps, and secondary editorial copy.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between structural, high-fashion brutality and quiet, effortless readability. 

- **Display & Headings:** `Space Grotesk` introduces modern, sculptural geometry reminiscent of contemporary beauty journals and architectural lookbooks. Set display headlines with negative letter-spacing for editorial density.
- **Running Text:** `Inter` handles long-form hair consultation guides, service descriptions, and pricing specifications with neutral clarity.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Formatted in uppercase `Space Grotesk` with generous tracking (`0.1em` to `0.12em`) to mirror luxury salon packaging, treatment numbers, and fragrance notes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetrical, magazine-inspired 12-column grid that shifts into tailored single-column streams on mobile screens.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with generous `2.5rem` gutters and `4rem` lateral margins, accommodating wide editorial imagery juxtaposed against tight, justified service menus.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins.
- **Rhythm:** Vertical pacing employs wide pauses (`space-xl` and above) between curated looks, client transformations, and booking flows, maintaining a calm, unhurried studio experience.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glass refractions and tonal containment instead of stark drop shadows.

- **Alabaster Tiering (Light Mode):** Baseline depth relies on soft background shifts from `#FAF7F2` to `#E9DFCE` containers, punctuated with hairline borders in `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08)`.
- **Frosted Liquid Glass:** Translucent floating booking bars, consultation navigation, and sticky headers apply `backdrop-filter: blur(18px)` over `rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.75)` with an inner rim highlight of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
- **Deep Espresso Immersion (Dark Elements & Footers):** Over `#1C1917` canvases, surfaces elevate through layered charcoal-bronze slabs (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`) with warm amber edge lighting (`1px solid rgba(155, 66, 43, 0.25)`) and diffused amber ground glows (`box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(155, 66, 43, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The form language balances architectural tailoring with organic, welcoming radii. Standard containers and interactive cards leverage subtle, tactile curvatures (`0.5rem`), while floating action elements, treatment pills, and status tags use pill forms (`9999px`) to echo salon stones, cosmetic vials, and smooth clay vessels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Booking / Signature Action):** Solid `#9B422B` fill with `#FAF7F2` text. Hover transitions to deep burnt sienna (`#843723`) with a soft ambient shadow tinted with warm terracotta.
- **Secondary (Editorial / Inquire):** Subtle `#E9DFCE` linen fill with `#1C1917` text and an invisible border that shifts to `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.15)` on hover.
- **Dark Surface Variant:** Deep Espresso backgrounds feature buttons in `#E9DFCE` with `#1C1917` typography, or frosted champagne glass outlines with amber hover glow.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- **Treatment & Lookbook Cards:** Built on `#FAF7F2` or `#F4EDE4` with crisp 1px borders of `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.06)`. Imagery uses natural editorial aspect ratios (4:5 or 3:4) with warm desaturation transitions on hover.
- **Featured Artisan / Master Stylist Cards:** Dark-mode cards grounded in `#1C1917` with subtle golden-champagne rim lines, displaying services with monospace treatment timestamps and clean price callouts.

### Input Fields & Booking Selectors
- **Inputs:** Clean linen-washed troughs (`#F4EDE4`) with bottom border or hairline outer frame. Focused state triggers a warm terracotta (`#9B422B`) border and an ambient `rgba(155, 66, 43, 0.08)` glow.
- **Date & Slot Pickers:** Compact pill-shaped toggle buttons; active slots invert into `#9B422B` with crisp white numerals, while unavailable slots sit in muted taupe with diagonal slashes.

### Chips & Filters
- **Category Filter Pills:** Upper-case micro-labels set inside pill containers. Unselected: `#FAF7F2` with `1px solid rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.12)`. Selected: `#1C1917` background with `#FAF7F2` text.

### Lookbook Gallery & Consultation Drawer
- **Drawer Panels:** Slide-in consultation assessments rendered in frosted alabaster glass (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.88)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and fine linen border dividers.
